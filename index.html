I’m passionate about automotive embedded software. I write C and C++ for microcontrollers and Raspberry Pi, and I built an ECU network simulator that talks over a CAN bus with OBD-II diagnostics. I test, debug and document everything I develop.

Selected projects

Personal project

Automotive ECU Network and Diagnostic Simulator

A C simulator of an ECU network (engine, dashboard) over a CAN bus, with a virtual engine ECU running a 10 Hz control cycle and OBD-II fault codes. Fault injection and pytest tests are in progress. C11, GCC, Git, Python/pytest.

Personal project

Traffic Sign Detection

A Raspberry Pi 4 system that detects and recognizes road signs in real time, from camera capture to classification. Tested in real conditions for lighting, distance and angle.

Personal project

Smart Soap Dispenser

Sensors and actuators connected through a microcontroller to Firebase, with live supervision of distribution data and equipment status in a Flutter app and a website.

FPGA Simulation and Programming

Digital circuits simulated and programmed on an FPGA board, including the design and simulation of a simplified RISC microprocessor in VHDL and Verilog.
